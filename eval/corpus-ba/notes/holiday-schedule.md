# Holiday schedule

The company observes a fixed set of public holidays each year, and the calendar is published at the start of the year by the people team. On those days the operations rota runs with a reduced staff list, and non-urgent changes are postponed to the next working day.

## Staffing
During holidays the primary on-call engineer keeps the pager, and the secondary is available by phone. Handover times may move, and the change is announced in the operations channel. The normal rotation is described in [[ops/oncall]].

## Deploys
Production deploys are not scheduled on holidays or on the working day before a holiday, as stated in [[ops/deploy-window]].

## Questions
Questions about the calendar go to the people team. Questions about the operations rota go to the platform lead.

## Bridge days
Bridge days between a holiday and a weekend are announced separately. The operations rota treats them like holidays for staffing purposes, but the deploy freeze applies only when a holiday falls on a working day.
