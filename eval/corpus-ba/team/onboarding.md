# Team onboarding

New engineers join in a two-week programme. The first week covers the architecture overview, the operations runbooks and the on-call shadowing schedule. The second week focuses on a small change in the adapter layer, which is reviewed by a buddy from the platform team.

## Accounts
Accounts are requested through the identity process. Access to production is not granted during onboarding and follows the quarterly review in [[security/access-review]].

## Reading list
Start with the queue design in [[architecture/larkspur-queue]] and the decision history in the ADR folder. The local setup steps are kept in the testing guide, [[guides/testing]].

## First week checklist
Read the rollout policy, attend one deploy, and sit in on one handover. The handover procedure is in [[ops/oncall]].

## Mentorship
Each new engineer has a buddy for the first month. The buddy reviews the first three pull requests and joins the first handover with them. Feedback from the buddy is shared with the manager at the end of the month.
