# Disaster recovery

## Procedure
The team uses this guide when working on disaster recovery. Before making a change, identify the owner and inspect the current system state. The main operational step is to document recovery objectives and rehearse failover with operators. If the check fails, stop the rollout and record the evidence for investigation.

## Verification
Repeat the check in a representative environment before approving production use. Record the outcome and the responsible operator in the change history. Review the procedure again after an incident or a change in dependencies.
