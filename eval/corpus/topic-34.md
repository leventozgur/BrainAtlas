# Transport protocol

## Procedure
The team uses this guide when working on transport protocol. Before making a change, identify the owner and inspect the current system state. The main operational step is to use encrypted connections and verify peer certificates. If the check fails, stop the rollout and record the evidence for investigation.

## Verification
Repeat the check in a representative environment before approving production use. Record the outcome and the responsible operator in the change history. Review the procedure again after an incident or a change in dependencies.
