# Deployment rollout

## Procedure
The team uses this guide when working on deployment rollout. Before making a change, identify the owner and inspect the current system state. The main operational step is to release to a small cohort and compare error rates before proceeding. If the check fails, stop the rollout and record the evidence for investigation.

## Verification
Repeat the check in a representative environment before approving production use. Record the outcome and the responsible operator in the change history. Review the procedure again after an incident or a change in dependencies.
